1. Who we are

Driver's Pay helps rideshare and delivery drivers work out what they actually earn once petrol and running costs are taken out, and estimate how their pay compares with the minimum standard for delivery workers in Australia.

The app is developed by Mareen Usliyanage ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app stores, where it's kept and what you can do about it.

2. What the app stores

Your work sessions

For each session you add, import or edit:

  • the date, or start and end dates for a longer period
  • the platform (Uber, DoorDash or Other)
  • hours worked and, for sessions imported from a screenshot, engaged time (time spent on jobs)
  • earnings, and how much of them came from promotions such as quests, boosts and adjustments
  • number of trips or deliveries
  • distance driven
  • fuel economy, petrol price, running costs and other expenses
  • any notes you write
  • when the session was created and last changed

Your settings

Preferences that set up your figures: your car's fuel economy, petrol price and running costs, hour limits, your main platform, currency symbol, distance unit (kilometres or miles), weekly earnings goal, what you deliver on (which sets the minimum rate that applies to you), the length and start date of your platform's earnings period, your appearance setting and whether you've finished setup.

What the app doesn't store or ask for

  • Your name, email address, phone number or any account
  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your contacts, camera, microphone or the rest of your photo library
  • Your Uber, DoorDash or other platform logins
  • Bank or payment details
  • Advertising identifiers, analytics or usage tracking

3. Where it's stored

Everything listed above is stored only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. It isn't sent to us or to any server, and the app works without an internet connection.

Earnings screenshots

You can import an Uber "Earnings activity" screenshot instead of typing your figures in. You choose the screenshot from your photos, and the app only receives that one image. The text on it is read on your phone, by Google ML Kit on Android or Apple's Vision framework on iOS. The image and the text read from it are not kept; only the day totals you choose to save become work sessions.

Phone backups

On Android, the app's data is excluded from Google's automatic backups. On iPhone, if you use iCloud Backup or back up your phone to a computer, that backup can include the app's data; those backups are controlled by you and Apple.

Exports

If you use Export sessions, the app creates a spreadsheet (CSV) file and opens your phone's share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it.

4. How it's used

Only on your phone, to:

  • show your sessions and work out your actual earnings after petrol and costs, your hourly and per-trip figures, and progress towards your goals
  • estimate how your pay compares with the minimum standard for each earnings period

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't build profiles of you. We can't see your data.

5. Who it's shared with

No one. The only thing that may leave your phone is:

  • Google ML Kit (Android only), which reads screenshots on your phone, may send Google limited diagnostic information about the text-recognition feature itself, such as device model, app version and error codes. It does not send your screenshot or the text read from it.

Apple's Vision framework, used on iOS, is part of the operating system and runs on your phone.

  • We never sell, rent or trade your data, and we don't receive it.
  • The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't share anything with Uber, DoorDash or any other platform, or with the Fair Work Commission, the ATO or any other government agency.

6. Deleting your data

In the app: go to Settings > Delete all data and confirm. Every session and setting on the phone is deleted immediately and permanently, and the app starts setup again. You can also delete single sessions at any time.

By uninstalling: removing the app deletes everything it stored.

We don't hold a copy, so there's nothing for us to delete on our side. More detail is on the data deletion page.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data. Everything you've entered is shown in the app, and you can edit or delete any session, and change any setting, at any time.
  • Take a copy. Use Settings > Export sessions to get all of your sessions as a spreadsheet. Because nothing is backed up by us, exporting is the way to keep a copy before changing phones.
  • Ask us. Questions about this policy are welcome; we'll reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how the app handles your data, please contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

8. Security

Your data is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps can't read. Anyone holding your unlocked phone can open the app, so keep a screen lock on your phone. If you think you've found a problem, please tell us.

9. About the estimates

The Earnings Guarantee figures, top-up amounts, actual earnings and hourly figures are estimates. They are worked out from the figures you enter or import and from our reading of the minimum standards for on-demand delivery workers. They are not financial, legal or tax advice, and what you are actually paid is decided by your platform.

Driver's Pay is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Uber, DoorDash, the Fair Work Commission or the Australian Government.

10. Children

Driver's Pay is for working drivers and is not intended for anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this policy
