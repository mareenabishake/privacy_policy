1. Who we are

Driver's Pay helps rideshare and delivery drivers work out what they actually earn once fuel and vehicle costs are taken out, and estimate how their pay compares with the minimum standard for delivery workers in Australia.

The app is developed by Mareen Usliyanage ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the app handles, where it goes and what you can do about it.

2. What we collect

Your account

  • Anonymous account. When you first open the app, it creates an account for you automatically, so you can start straight away. This account has a random user ID and no name or email address.
  • Email (optional). If you choose to back up your data with an email address, we store that address. You also set a password; it is handled by our sign-in provider, Supabase, which stores it only in a scrambled (hashed) form that we cannot read. Supabase sends you an email to confirm the address.
  • Google sign-in (optional). If you link or sign in with a Google account, Google shares your Google account's email address with our sign-in provider, along with the basic profile details Google provides, such as your name and profile picture link. The app doesn't use or display your name or picture.
  • Sign-in records. When your account was created and when you last signed in.

Your work sessions

For each session you add, import or edit:

  • the date, or start and end dates for a longer period
  • the platform (Uber, DoorDash or Other)
  • hours worked and, for sessions imported from a screenshot, engaged time (time spent on jobs)
  • earnings, and how much of them came from promotions such as quests, boosts and adjustments
  • number of trips or deliveries
  • distance driven
  • fuel economy, fuel price, vehicle running cost per kilometre and other expenses
  • any notes you write
  • when the session was created and last changed

Your settings

Preferences that set up your figures: hour limits, default fuel and running-cost figures, your main platform, currency symbol, distance unit (kilometres or miles), daily and weekly earnings goals, what you deliver on (which sets the minimum rate that applies to you), and the length and start date of your platform's earnings period.

Technical information

Like any online service, our database provider's servers keep technical records of requests made to them, such as the time and the IP address a request came from. These are used to keep the service running and secure, not to track you.

What we don't collect

  • Your location or GPS data
  • Your contacts, camera, microphone or the rest of your photo library
  • Your Uber, DoorDash or other platform logins
  • Bank or payment details
  • Advertising identifiers, analytics or usage tracking

3. What stays on your phone

Earnings screenshots

You can import an Uber "Earnings activity" screenshot instead of typing your figures in. You choose the screenshot from your photos, and the app only receives that one image.

The text on the screenshot is read on your phone, by Google ML Kit on Android or Apple's Vision framework on iOS. The image, and the raw text read from it, are never uploaded to us or stored in your account. You then review the figures the app found, and only the day totals you choose to save (date, earnings, promotions, engaged time, trips and distance) are stored as work sessions.

Settings kept only on this phone

  • Your appearance setting (light, dark or match your phone).
  • Whether you have finished the setup screens.

The app also keeps a sign-in token on your phone so you stay signed in.

Exports

If you use Export sessions, the app creates a spreadsheet (CSV) file on your phone and opens your phone's share sheet. The file goes only where you choose to send it.

4. How we use your data

Only to run the app for you:

  • to show your sessions and calculate your profit after fuel and vehicle costs, your hourly and per-trip figures, and progress towards your goals
  • to estimate how your pay compares with the minimum standard for each earnings period
  • to keep your data safe on our servers, so a backed-up account can be restored on a new phone
  • to reply when you contact us

We don't use your data for advertising, and we don't build profiles of you.

5. Who we share it with

We use a small number of service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase stores your account, sessions and settings, and handles sign-in. Your data is hosted in Supabase's Sydney, Australia region. Data is encrypted in transit between the app and Supabase. Supabase is a US-based company acting as our service provider.
  • Google, only if you choose to sign in with Google. Google confirms who you are and shares the details described in section 2.
  • Google ML Kit (Android only) reads screenshots on your phone. ML Kit may send Google limited diagnostic information about the text-recognition feature itself, such as device model, app version and error codes. It does not send your screenshot or the text read from it.

Apple's Vision framework, used on iOS, is part of the operating system and runs on your phone.

  • We never sell, rent or trade your data.
  • We don't show ads, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking tools.
  • We don't share your data with Uber, DoorDash or any other platform, or with the Fair Work Commission, the ATO or any other government agency.

We would only disclose your data if the law required us to, for example under a valid court order.

6. How long we keep it

Your account, sessions and settings are kept until you delete them. Deleting a session in the app removes it straight away, and deleting your account removes everything (see below).

If you haven't backed up. An anonymous account can only be reached from the phone it was created on, because there is no email or Google account to sign back in with. If you sign out, uninstall the app or lose your phone without backing up, the data stays stored but neither you nor we can link it back to you. If you want it removed, delete your account in the app before you uninstall.

7. Deleting your account

In the app: go to Settings > Delete account and confirm. Your account, all of your work sessions and all of your settings are deleted immediately and permanently.

By email: if you can't use the app, email mareen.abishake@gmail.com from the address on your account. An anonymous account has no email address, so it can only be deleted from inside the app.

Full details are on the account deletion page.

8. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data. Everything you've entered is shown in the app, and you can edit or delete any session, and change any setting, at any time.
  • Take a copy. Use Settings > Export sessions to get all of your sessions as a spreadsheet.
  • Use it without an email. Backing up with an email or Google account is optional.
  • Ask us. You can email us to ask what we hold about you, to correct it or to delete it. We'll reply within 30 days.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, please contact us first. You can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner.

9. Security

  • All data travels between the app and our servers over encrypted connections.
  • The database itself enforces that each account can only read and change its own sessions and settings. This is checked on the server, not just in the app.
  • Passwords are handled by Supabase and stored only in hashed form.

Anyone holding your unlocked phone can open the app, so keep a screen lock on your phone. No system is perfectly secure; if you think you've found a problem, please tell us.

10. About the estimates

The Earnings Guarantee figures, top-up amounts, profit and hourly figures are estimates. They are worked out from the figures you enter or import and from our reading of the minimum standards for on-demand delivery workers. They are not financial, legal or tax advice, and what you are actually paid is decided by your platform.

Driver's Pay is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Uber, DoorDash, the Fair Work Commission or the Australian Government.

11. Children

Driver's Pay is for working drivers and is not intended for anyone under 18. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we'll delete the account.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we'll update this page and the effective date at the top.
