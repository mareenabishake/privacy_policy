Driver's Pay

Delete your Driver's Pay data

Driver's Pay keeps everything on your phone and has no accounts, so you delete your data on the phone itself.

  • App: Driver's Pay (Android package com.driverspay.app)
  • Developer: Mareen Usliyanage
Quickest

Delete in the app

  1. Open Driver's Pay.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete all data.
  4. Confirm. Every session and setting is deleted straight away, and the app starts setup again.

Want a copy first? Use Settings > Export sessions to save your sessions as a spreadsheet before you delete. Once deleted, the data can't be recovered.

Alternative

Uninstall the app

Removing Driver's Pay from your phone deletes everything it stored.

What gets deleted

  • All of your work sessions: dates, platforms, hours, engaged time, earnings, promotions, trips, distance, petrol and cost figures, and notes.
  • All of your settings: car costs, goals, vehicle type, earnings period and display preferences.

Immediate and permanent. This can't be undone.

What we keep

Nothing. Driver's Pay doesn't send your sessions or settings to us, so we have no copy to delete. If your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups include the app, those backups are controlled by you and Apple.
