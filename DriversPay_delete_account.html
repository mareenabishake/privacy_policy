Delete your Driver's Pay account

You can delete your account and all of its data at any time, in the app or by email.

  • App: Driver's Pay (Android package com.driverspay.app)
  • Developer: Mareen Usliyanage
Quickest

Delete in the app

  1. Open Driver's Pay.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all of its data are deleted straight away.

Want a copy first? Use Settings > Export sessions to save your sessions as a spreadsheet before you delete. Once your account is deleted, the data can't be recovered.

Alternative

Ask by email

If you can't use the app (for example, you no longer have the phone), send an email from the address on your Driver's Pay account. If you signed in with Google, use that Google account's email address.

To
mareen.abishake@gmail.com
Subject
Delete my Driver's Pay account

We'll delete the account within 30 days of your request and reply to let you know it's done.

Never backed up? If you have only used the app without adding an email or Google account, your account has no email address, so we have no way to confirm that a request is yours. Please delete it from inside the app instead.

What gets deleted

  • Your account: its user ID, email address, password and any linked Google sign-in.
  • All of your work sessions: dates, platforms, hours, engaged time, earnings, promotions, trips, distance, fuel and cost figures, and notes.
  • All of your settings: default costs, goals, vehicle type, earnings period and display preferences.

Immediate and permanent. Your sessions and settings are linked to your account in the database, so deleting the account deletes all of them with it, at the same moment. This can't be undone.

Your appearance setting and setup status are stored only on your phone, never on our servers. Uninstalling the app removes them.

What we keep

We don't keep a copy of your account, sessions or settings after they're deleted, and nothing is held back unless the law requires it.

Our hosting provider keeps routine technical logs of requests to the service, such as the time and IP address of a sign-in, for security. These aren't part of your account data and aren't used for anything else.
